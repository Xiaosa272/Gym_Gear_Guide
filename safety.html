Train with Care

Share the gym safely.

Use a weight you can control. Keep the floor clear. Leave the next student a clean place.

Six Simple Rules

Before, during and after

  1. Warm up before hard exercise.
  2. Check the seat, belt or weight first.
  3. Use a light setting when you are new.
  4. Keep water away from electric parts.
  5. Clean the handles after use.
  6. Put weights back in the right place.
What should I do if a machine looks broken?

Do not use it. Tell school staff and choose another machine.

Can I train for two hours?

Yes, but take a short break. Drink water and change your movement.

What if I do not know how to use the equipment?

Ask a teacher or trained friend. Do not guess when a heavy weight is involved.

Why should I put the weights back?

A clear floor is safer. It also helps the next person find the right weight.
